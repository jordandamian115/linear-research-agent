# Tell me a little bit about the moon

## Abstract
This paper asks tell me a little bit about the moon? It is written so a reader can talk through the basics: what the subject is, the core ideas in the order the records support, how each idea leads to the next, and where the account stops. A later sentence is used only when it adds that next point. Claims stay inside the sentences the search returned.

## Introduction
The question guiding this paper is: tell me a little bit about the moon?
