## What the records support
Quantum physics is the study of matter and energy at the most fundamental level. [1] The same record continues: It aims to uncover the properties and behaviors of the very building blocks of nature. [1] The next record strengthens that opening: Quantum physics is a fascinating branch of science that explores the behavior of matter and energy at the smallest scales of our universe. [2] The same record continues: Quantum physics is the foundation of much of the technology we use today, and its principles shape our understanding of the universe at the smallest scales. [2] From what the subject is, the records go next to how matter and energy are divided.

The contrast needs a first idea. The next record says how these phenomena are divided into units. Among the basic discoveries was the realization that matter and energy can be thought of as discrete packets, or quanta, that have a minimum value associated with them. [1] From how matter and energy are divided, the records go next to wave and particle behavior.

Those units are the start, not the finish. The next record says they are not particles alone. Quantum mechanics is the field of physics that explains how extremely small objects simultaneously have the characteristics of both particles (tiny pieces of matter) and waves (a disturbance or variation that transfers energy). [3] From wave and particle behavior, the records go next to probability in place of a single certain path.

The next record says what can be claimed about where a particle is. Modern quantum physics instead understands electrons as being distributed within orbitals, mathematical descriptions that represent the probability of the electrons' existence in more than one location within a given range at any given time. [1] Stop after that sentence when you explain this. The records do not supply a further step, and this paper does not invent one.
